# Commercial strategy and search plan

Research reviewed 20 October 2026. The supplied BCN research PDF was read before implementation. This update follows the owner's later instruction: no visible promotion, linking or sign-in for the separate card product.

## Positioning

**Real help for real business owners.** Work out a decision, finish a task, then choose whether to ask a relevant person. Start with independent UK service owners rather than trying to compete across every business topic and country at once.

The differentiated experience is a connected owner journey, not simply another calculator or a broad networking feed. This is a commercial hypothesis, not measured product-market fit.

## Revenue priorities

1. **Consented service introductions.** Begin with website, bookkeeping, design or operational help. Approve provider capability, response process, capacity, complaints contact and written fee terms before matching. Record a potential fee separately; count revenue only against actual receipt evidence. No provider sale is automatically BCN revenue. A related business needs its own disclosed arrangement.
2. **Relevant software referrals.** Publish an original decision guide only where the software helps a specific problem. Record programme approval, destination, permitted marketing methods and disclosure. Publish no commission amount unless it is contractually verified for BCN. Compare alternatives and total cost independently of commission.
3. **Optional useful packs, after demand.** Candidate products: a job-cost tracker with variance analysis, a client handover pack, or a scope/change-control pack. Indicative £9–£29 testing range from the prior strategy is a proposed experiment, not a current price or revenue forecast. Keep the free quote PDF and complete calculator results free.
4. **Later, with audience evidence.** Clearly labelled tool sponsorship or newsletter sponsorship; then optional promoted listings, a workshop or white-label calculator. Avoid display-ad clutter, an unmoderated job board and paid trust badges.

## Journeys and intent hypotheses

| Owner's question | Entry | Useful result | Optional next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What should I charge for a service job? | Pricing guide / job calculator | Costs, margin, VAT and total | Print a quote; ask for relevant operational help |
| How many jobs cover my costs? | Break-even guide / calculator | Whole sales and capacity reality | Review price or costs |
| Is a cheaper monthly subscription actually cheaper? | Software guide / comparison | Like-for-like total over a selected period | Read a relevant disclosed recommendation, once approved |
| Why does my website get few enquiries? | Website guide / manual checklist | Three concrete checks to perform | Private website-help request |
| How do I write a clear quote? | Quote guide / builder | Editable scope, line items and print/PDF | Save locally and agree terms with the customer |

These are plausible problem intents, not verified search-volume, competition or conversion data. Public search results currently expose legacy BCN `/home`, membership, about, audit, contact and intent pages. This establishes discoverability only. Search Console traffic, rankings and backlink metrics were not accessible; no traffic baseline or backlink-value estimate is invented.

## Search and AI discovery

- Keep useful existing insight URLs. Revised positioning pages retain their paths. `/home` and join/device entry routes use reversible redirects during review; approve permanent redirects only after checking Search Console and equivalence.
- New pages use descriptive titles, canonical URLs, crawlable content and internal links. Free tool pages use `WebApplication` structured data with a zero-price offer. No invented ratings, reviews or guarantees.
- Publish a small set of useful guides with worked examples, current official sources and an identifiable review process. The six new guides are editorial drafts for owner review, not presented as professional advice.
- [Google's AI guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) states normal SEO fundamentals remain relevant and no special optimisation is required. BCN makes no promise of rankings or AI citations. `llms.txt` is informational, not a ranking mechanism.
- Use Search Console after launch for index coverage, real queries and page-level decisions. Add content in response to observed needs, rather than generating large batches of thin pages.

## Practical decision gates

For the first controlled 90 days, observe completed tools, repeat use, genuine qualified help requests, successful introductions, collected BCN receipts, complaints and operator time. Establish numeric targets after obtaining a baseline. Do not buy traffic or add new monetisation surfaces merely because they are possible. Founder capacity and margin matter alongside revenue.
